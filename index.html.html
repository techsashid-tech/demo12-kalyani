Demo website policy — review before launch

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This page describes the current demo website; it must be reviewed and approved by Kalyani Charitable Trust before production use.

Information you choose to share

The contact form is processed in your browser and opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message. We do not run a server-side contact database or collect payment information on this demo. The message is sent only if you choose to send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own policies apply after you leave this website.

External services

This page loads Google Fonts, illustrative photos from Unsplash (and fallback photos from Picsum), a sample video from MDN, and links to Google Maps and WhatsApp. Those services may receive normal browser information such as your IP address and request details under their own policies. The website itself does not set analytics or marketing cookies.

Data retention and your choices

The demo does not store form entries. For messages you send through WhatsApp, contact the trust directly at +91 82777 54614 to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable law and platform limitations.

Children's privacy

Please do not submit a child's personal information without appropriate parent or guardian consent.

Changes and contact

The trust should provide its official address, email and approved privacy contact before launch. Until then, questions about this demo may be raised by calling +91 82777 54614.
